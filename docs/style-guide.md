# Isla's Salon: cel-shading style guide

Everything on screen is drawn in code (PixiJS Graphics). These rules keep the character, props and backdrop
looking like one cartoon. The constants live in `src/render/style.ts`.

## Outlines
- One ink colour for every outline: `INK` (#1d1030), never pure black.
- Large shapes (head, gown, hair silhouette, shelves, mirror): 4px (`OUTLINE`).
- Small props and the lines between hair locks: 3px (`OUTLINE_THIN`).
- Round joins and caps. Outlines are never dropped on a shape, even a small one.

## Colour
- Flat fills only: no gradients, no blur, no transparency except glass, blush and highlights.
- Vibrant, slightly candy-coloured palette (`PALETTE`). Pink and sky blue frame the scene; the black gown
  is the only dark mass, so keep it clean and let hair, skin and props pop against it.
- Skin is warm peach with pink blush. Hair uses whatever colour the player chose, shaded by rule (below).

## Light and shadow
- One light, fixed in screen space at the upper left (`LIGHT`). Turning Isla does not move the light.
- Every shape has exactly two tones: lit and shadow. Shadow = lit multiplied by `SHADOW_FACTOR` (0.78).
- The shadow is a hard-edged crescent on the lower right, made by drawing a slightly smaller lit shape on top
  of an outlined shadow-coloured shape (`celEllipse`). No soft edges.
- Hair: each lock is a ribbon; the left ~58% (screen space) is lit, the right ~42% is shadow. Overlapping
  locks give the stripe pattern. Alternate locks sit slightly inside the others and are a touch darker.
- A single curved highlight band sits on the upper left of the head, only where hair actually covers it.

## Hair colour
Hair can be one colour, split left and right, or ombre. The rules live in `src/render/colour.ts` and are all unit-tested.
- **Left and Right are always the player's.** Left (colour a) is the screen-left of the front view, Right (colour b) the screen-right, and from behind they swap on screen. The 'left' view turns Isla so the Left side of her head faces the player (her face points to the screen-right), and the 'right' view shows the other side. Cutting regions use the same rule. Nothing in the game uses Isla's own left and right.
- **Colour changes fade in over one second** (`COLOUR_FADE_SECONDS`, sine in-out). A change mid-fade starts from the
  colour on screen; with reduced motion it is instant, and the first colour on load never fades.
- **One colour**: every lock the same colour, with the usual lit and shadow tones.
- **Left and right**: each lock takes its colour from the side of the head it grows from, so the join runs down
  the middle of the front parting and the back, and stays correct as Isla turns.
- **Ombre**: roots to tips, per lock, in flat bands (`OMBRE_STEPS` + 1 of them, never a smooth fade, to keep the
  cel-shaded look). The top third or so of each lock keeps the roots colour, then it steps towards the tips
  colour. Bands are measured along each lock, so both colours show at every length, down to the shortest crop.
- **Mixing colours** goes round the hue circle by the shorter way (in OKLab), so blonde to purple passes through
  orange and pink rather than turning muddy grey.
- Each tone is made from the band colour the same way as for any other shape: shadow is lit x 0.78, and
  alternate locks are x 0.95. Outlines stay the single ink colour, drawn once per lock so they run unbroken
  down it, whatever bands lie underneath.
- Eyebrows take a darker shade of the root colour on their own side of the head, and the highlight band takes
  the colour of the hair it lies on.
- The quick-pick swatches (`HAIR_SWATCHES`) are a few natural shades first, then the vibrant ones. The chooser
  also has a mixer (hue, vividness, lightness) and the device's own colour picker.

## Faces
- Big round eyes with a white, a coloured iris, a dark pupil and one white sparkle. Small nose, simple smile,
  rosy cheeks, thin darker-than-hair eyebrows.
- In profile only the near eye shows, narrowed by foreshortening, and the nose pokes out of the silhouette.

## Props
- Built from simple rounded shapes with the thin outline. Plants, pots, bottles, scissors, combs, brushes,
  hairdryer, clippers, clips, colour bowl and tool jar are generated by `src/render/props.ts`, so scenes
  vary by position, size and colour rather than by new art.
- Wash scene: tiled wall, sink, tap, potted plants. Colour/Cut/Style scene: mirror, two shelves of tools,
  potted plants.

## Hair rules (what the model guarantees)
- Hair is a set of locks hanging from the scalp, so it can be seen correctly from all four views.
- Four regions (front, left, right, back), six lengths each; lengths blend smoothly between regions.
- Long front hair is swept to the sides so Isla's eyes, brows and cheeks are never covered.
- Short front lengths are fringes that stop above the eyebrows.

## Cutting
- Six lengths, longest first: Long, Shoulder, Bob, Short, Shorter, Crop. Each of the four regions (front, left, right, back) has its own length, and the reach blends smoothly round the head between region centres, so seams never step.
- **Left and Right regions are the player's** (Left = screen-left of the front view, a negative azimuth), matching the colour chooser and the views. The mapping lives in `src/cut.ts`.
- A shorter cut plays the scissors: they arrive, snip twice (`playSnip`), then the hair drops and clippings fall in the tip colour. A longer cut grows with a soft chime (`playGrow`). Reduced motion skips the animation.
- Sound is synthesised with Web Audio in `src/audio.ts`; nothing is loaded and mute silences it all.

## Washing
- Sink scene: tiled wall, handheld sprayer aimed at her crown, a temperature dial, shampoos and conditioner on a wall shelf, a towel shelf and plants. The dial and bottles are part of the picture and respond to touch.
- Each shampoo has its own look (`SCENT` in `washfx.ts`): mint is pale green lather with leaves, citrus warm yellow with droplets, tropical pink with petals. Conditioner is a soft white-lilac cream.
- Water is tinted by temperature: icy blue with frost when cold, clear when warm, blushed with rising steam when hot. Pressure changes the number, speed, width and fan of the streaks.
- Clean, silky, rinsed hair sparkles. All washing sound is synthesised in `src/audio.ts` and follows mute.

## Styling
- A `Look` (`src/render/look.ts`) is three numbers: curl 0 to 3, spiky 0 to 1, flat 0 to 1. The scene tweens them over 0.6s and rebuilds the hair each frame, so any in-between look has to be valid hair too (tested).
- **Curl** swings each lock sideways and radially (more and faster with each level), adds fullness, and shortens hanging hair by up to 21% at the tightest.
- **Spiky** lifts the crown in tufts of three different heights and flicks the tips out and narrows them; half as strong on the longest cuts.
- **Flat** thins the hair to a sleek skin over the head, removes the natural wave and 85% of any curl.
- Curling gets stars and a boing; product gets mist and a hiss (`src/render/stylefx.ts`, `src/audio.ts`).

## Photo and polish
- The photo is the 400 x 500 design box (the picture of Isla and the salon) rendered at 1080 x 1350, so it is always 4:5. Controls, cut markers, scissors, clippings and sparks are hidden while it is taken, eyes are open and the breathing lift is removed.
- Idle life: a blink every 2.4 to 5.4 seconds (0.18s), and a 2.2px breathing lift over 2.1s, in steps of 0.2px so it costs few frames. Both are off with reduced motion or `?still`.
- Small pink text uses `--pink-deep` (#c02479), which has a contrast ratio of 5.2 on the cream panel.

## Release
- The app icon is Isla's face with scissors on the salon pink (`public/icon.svg`); the theme colour is the same pink.
- Round buttons run in a column down the right of the picture and start a second column when the picture is short.
- Anything that doesn't fit the screen scrolls inside its panel; the page itself never scrolls.
