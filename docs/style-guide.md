# Isla's Salon: cel-shading style guide

Everything on screen is drawn in code (PixiJS Graphics). These rules keep the character, props and backdrop
looking like one cartoon. The constants live in `src/render/style.ts`.

## Outlines
- One ink colour for every outline: `INK` (#1d1030), never pure black.
- Large shapes (head, gown, hair silhouette, shelves, mirror): 4px (`OUTLINE`).
- Small props and the lines between hair locks: 3px (`OUTLINE_THIN`).
- Round joins and caps. Outlines are never dropped on a shape, even a small one.

## Colour
- Flat fills only: no gradients, no blur, no transparency except glass, blush and highlights.
- Vibrant, slightly candy-coloured palette (`PALETTE`). Pink and sky blue frame the scene; the black gown
  is the only dark mass, so keep it clean and let hair, skin and props pop against it.
- Skin is warm peach with pink blush. Hair uses whatever colour the player chose, shaded by rule (below).

## Light and shadow
- One light, fixed in screen space at the upper left (`LIGHT`). Turning Isla does not move the light.
- Every shape has exactly two tones: lit and shadow. Shadow = lit multiplied by `SHADOW_FACTOR` (0.78).
- The shadow is a hard-edged crescent on the lower right, made by drawing a slightly smaller lit shape on top
  of an outlined shadow-coloured shape (`celEllipse`). No soft edges.
- Hair: each lock is a ribbon; the left ~58% (screen space) is lit, the right ~42% is shadow. Overlapping
  locks give the stripe pattern. Alternate locks sit slightly inside the others and are a touch darker.
- A single curved highlight band sits on the upper left of the head, only where hair actually covers it.

## Faces
- Big round eyes with a white, a coloured iris, a dark pupil and one white sparkle. Small nose, simple smile,
  rosy cheeks, thin darker-than-hair eyebrows.
- In profile only the near eye shows, narrowed by foreshortening, and the nose pokes out of the silhouette.

## Props
- Built from simple rounded shapes with the thin outline. Plants, pots, bottles, scissors, combs, brushes,
  hairdryer, clippers, clips, colour bowl and tool jar are generated by `src/render/props.ts`, so scenes
  vary by position, size and colour rather than by new art.
- Wash scene: tiled wall, sink, tap, potted plants. Colour/Cut/Style scene: mirror, two shelves of tools,
  potted plants.

## Hair rules (what the model guarantees)
- Hair is a set of locks hanging from the scalp, so it can be seen correctly from all four views.
- Four regions (front, left, right, back), six lengths each; lengths blend smoothly between regions.
- Long front hair is swept to the sides so Isla's eyes, brows and cheeks are never covered.
- Short front lengths are fringes that stop above the eyebrows.
